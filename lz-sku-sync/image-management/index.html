    Image Management¶

    Configure how product images are synchronized from Zoho Inventory to WooCommerce.

    Overview¶

    LZ SKU Sync automatically handles product images:

    • ✅ Downloads images from Zoho Inventory
    • ✅ Uploads to WordPress Media Library
    • ✅ Generates standard WordPress thumbnails
    • ✅ Prevents duplicate images
    • ✅ Assigns images to products and variations
    • ✅ Manages gallery images for variable products

    Note

    Image processing happens in the background to prevent timeouts. You can close your browser - images will continue downloading.

    Image Sync Settings¶

    Control which images are synchronized:

    Product Image (Featured)¶

    • Main product image shown on product listings
    • First image from Zoho becomes featured image
    • Enable/disable in Sync Status > Sync Preferences

    Gallery Images¶

    • Additional product images
    • Displayed in product image carousel
    • Enable/disable in Sync Status > Sync Preferences

    Note

    Both options can be enabled/disabled independently in Product Settings.

    Variable Product Images¶

    For variable products (product groups in Zoho):

    • Each variation gets its own images
    • Parent product automatically inherits variation images
    • Duplicates are automatically filtered out

    Image Requirements¶

    Zoho Inventory¶

    • Images must be attached to items in Zoho Inventory
    • Supported formats: JPG, PNG, GIF, WebP
    • Maximum recommended size: 5MB per image
    • Multiple images per product supported

    WordPress¶

    • GD or Imagick PHP extension recommended
    • Uploads directory must be writable
    • Sufficient disk space for images

    Best Practices¶

    Before Syncing¶

    1. ✅ Upload high-quality images to Zoho Inventory
    2. ✅ Use descriptive image filenames
    3. ✅ Remove unnecessary images from Zoho to speed up sync

    After Sync¶

    1. ✅ Review products to ensure images are correct
    2. ✅ Delete unused media via Media > Library (if needed)
    3. ✅ Use image optimization plugins for WebP/compression (optional)

    Troubleshooting Images¶

    Images Not Appearing¶

    Solutions:

    • ✅ Verify images exist in Zoho Inventory (Items → Attachments)
    • ✅ Check "Product Image" is enabled in sync preferences
    • ✅ Check Sync Log for errors
    • ✅ Contact your hosting provider about upload permissions

    Images Taking Long to Sync¶

    This is normal - images are rate-limited to comply with Zoho API limits. For large catalogs, image sync may take 10-20 minutes. Images continue downloading even if you close your browser.

    Low Quality Images¶

    Upload higher resolution images to Zoho Inventory. The plugin downloads images at their original quality.
